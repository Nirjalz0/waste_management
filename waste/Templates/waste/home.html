From full bin to filed report, in three steps

No separate systems to reconcile. Sensor data, route logic, and compliance records all live in the same pipeline.

01 — Sense

Bins report themselves

Ultrasonic sensors in bins and dumpsters send fill-level readings every hour, so crews stop guessing and stop driving to bins that are half-empty.

02 — Route

Routes rebuild overnight

Each morning's route is generated from what's actually full, truck capacity, and road restrictions — not last year's static map.

03 — Report

Diversion, logged automatically

Tonnage, contamination flags, and missed-pickup exceptions roll up into the reports your municipality or client already requires.

Built for the whole operation, not just the trucks

Dispatchers, drivers, and residents each get the view they need — pulling from the same live data.

Dispatch

A live map that reroutes with you

Drag a stop, flag a blocked street, or add a same-day pickup — the rest of the route adjusts automatically and every driver's tablet updates in seconds.

Driver app
